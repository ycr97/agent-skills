# Understanding Rubric

Use this rubric to estimate the learner's current capability and choose the next teaching move.

## L0 — Recognition

The learner recognizes terminology or says they have seen the concept before, but cannot explain it reliably.

**Next move:** establish definitions and prerequisites.

## L1 — Recall

The learner can reproduce a definition, rule, or memorized conclusion.

**Failure signature:** explanation collapses when wording changes.

**Next move:** build causal structure and a mental model.

## L2 — Explanation

The learner can explain the main mechanism in their own words and connect cause to effect.

**Failure signature:** cannot reliably predict altered conditions.

**Next move:** prediction questions and counterexamples.

## L3 — Prediction

The learner can use the model to predict what happens when one or more relevant variables change.

**Failure signature:** works on textbook examples but not realistic problems.

**Next move:** applied scenarios with incomplete or noisy information.

## L4 — Application

The learner can diagnose or solve unfamiliar problems using the principle without step-by-step prompting.

**Failure signature:** still depends heavily on surface similarity to prior examples.

**Next move:** transfer test across a different context.

## L5 — Transfer

The learner recognizes the same deep principle in a different-looking situation and can adapt it correctly.

This is the strongest practical evidence of durable understanding.

## Diagnostic dimensions

When a learner fails, classify the failure before teaching more:

| Dimension | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Facts | Is a prerequisite fact missing or incorrect? |
| Model | Is the learner's internal representation wrong or incomplete? |
| Causality | Is there a broken cause-and-effect link? |
| Boundaries | Is a valid rule being applied outside its domain? |
| Discrimination | Is the learner confusing two similar concepts? |
| Transfer | Is the learner overfitting to the original example? |

Use the earliest failing dimension as the repair target.
